This chapter provides a short, high-level summary of the overall thesis findings.
Detailed results for the neighborhood-size sweep (including metrics, per-simulation diagnostics, and ablations) and for the curriculum learning experiment are reported in \cref{c:implementation}, because they are tightly connected to the experimental pipeline design.

\section{Overall findings}
\label{sec:results:overall}

Across all experiments, we find that:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Neighborhood size matters, but mainly at the first step:} moving from \(NB=1\) to \(NB\ge2\) yields a large improvement in stability and quality.
\item \textbf{Bigger is not always better:} for \(NB\ge2\), performance does not improve monotonically and depends on the metric and rollout horizon.
\item \textbf{Evaluation protocol is critical:} differences across neighborhood sizes are easiest to interpret when training and evaluation are consistent (sampling/temperature choices) and when models are assessed at multiple rollout horizons.
\end{itemize}

\section{Thesis takeaway}
\label{sec:results:takeaway}

The main takeaway of this thesis is that MNCA can model tissue-like dynamics in a controlled synthetic setting, but careful experimental design is required to obtain interpretable conclusions.
In particular, the neighborhood-size effect can be masked by training/evaluation confounds when models already fail at long-horizon rollouts.
Under the controlled sweep (Step~3), KL divergence and the other biological metrics show that \(NB \ge 2\) is significantly better than \(NB=1\), with strong statistical evidence (Kruskal--Wallis \(p < 10^{-4}\) and post-hoc tests); beyond that, no single \(NB\) is best on every metric.
In the curriculum experiment (\cref{sec:impl:curriculum}), the stochastic variant with \(NB=5\) attains the best long-horizon distributional metrics at 100 and 500 steps (e.g., categorical MMD around 0.04--0.05 at \(NB=5\)), while the deterministic mixture exhibits a metric- and horizon-dependent trade-off and often degrades at long rollouts.
All setting, qualitative examples, and quantitative results for both experiments are described in the Implementation chapter (\cref{c:implementation}); limitations and threats to validity are discussed in \cref{sec:impl:threats}.
